## Purpose and Scope of this Project

**LandOfTheDroid** is an independent, non-commercial, open-source compatibility layer and native Android port developed solely for:
1. **Digital Preservation**: Ensuring that classic PC software remains accessible and playable on modern hardware architectures.
2. **Platform Interoperability**: Providing a modern 64-bit ARM (`arm64-v8a`) runtime, OpenGL ES 3.0 graphics translation, and touch input controls for Android devices.
3. **Educational Research**: Demonstrating the static recompilation and modernization of legacy Unreal Engine 2 binaries.

This repository is distributed free of charge under open-source software licenses. It does not generate revenue, accept payments, or offer commercial licensing.

## No Distribution of Proprietary Game Assets

**LandOfTheDroid does NOT contain, distribute, host, or mirror any copyrighted proprietary game assets.**

The repository does not supply:
- Retail game levels or maps (`.dz`, `.unr`)
- Proprietary texture packages (`.utx`)
- Skeletal meshes or character models (`.ukx`)
- Sound effects, dialogue, or musical scores (`.uax`, `.ogg`)
- Pre-rendered cinematics or cutscene videos (`.bik`, `.mpg`)
- Original retail engine binaries or installation media

To utilize this software, users must legally acquire and supply their own copy of *Land of the Dead: Road to Fiddler's Green* from their own retail discs or authorized digital media.
